How to get chords from a YouTube video
  1. Open the video in another tab of Chrome or Edge on a computer.
  2. Click Listen to a tab, choose the YouTube tab, and keep Also share tab audio switched on.
  3. Play the video from the start. Chords appear below with their times.
  4. Click Copy chord sheet when the song ends.

On a phone, use Listen with mic and play the video on another device or a speaker.
